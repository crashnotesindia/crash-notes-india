Nine stones, one return: reading the Rockageddon ladder in rupees

Some games hide their maths. Rockageddon by Turbo Games, a Jewel Clicker instant game released on 1 October 2026, prints most of it on the rules panel. That makes it a good game to learn from, even if you never stake a rupee on it.

How a tap works

Stones drift across the field with multipliers painted on them. A tap on a stone is one bet. If the stone bursts, you are paid the stake times that multiplier at once, with the credit landing on the balance in about the time it takes to read the number. If the stone has already drifted and your tap lands on empty space, nothing is deducted. There is no cash-out button and no round clock.

Nine tiers, one number

Every stone belongs to one of nine tiers, and the rules panel lists both the multiplier and the win chance. The x1.2 stone hits 80% of the time. The x2 stone, 48%. The x8 stone, 12%. The x96 boulder, 1%. Multiply any pair and you get 0.96, which matches the 96% RTP the studio lists. On a ₹100 tap that means ₹120, ₹200, ₹800 or ₹9,600 when a stone bursts, and the long-run return is the same whichever you pick.

So the choice is not about value. It is about rhythm. Small stones give frequent small bumps. Big stones give long dry stretches that break only when a rare hit lands.

What a short session looked like

The practice notes on the destination page log about 75 taps across manual picks, START and two Autoplay blocks, scaled so one demo unit reads as ₹100. A Small and Medium block of 25 taps lost ₹660. A Big stones block hit x48 on its third tap for ₹4,800 and then missed 22 times in a row. The whole set finished ₹510 up, and without that single x48 it would have been negative. Miss streaks of 21 and 22 taps turned up in the same short run.

The button worth pricing first

Buy 100 Taps fires a hundred bets in seconds. At ₹100 a tap that is ₹10,000. At ₹50 it is ₹5,000. Autoplay blocks of 25 taps finish in under twenty seconds at roughly 0.7 seconds a tap, which is fast enough for a dry stretch to chew through a balance before you notice.

The tier table and the Autoplay zones are laid out in a Rockageddon breakdown showing the same 96% return on all nine stone tiers, along with the 10,000 max-profit cap on the free version.
