A Sunday in Oman time: following India U19 v Pakistan U19 from India

Youth cricket rarely gets a whole Sunday to itself in India, but India Under-19 against Pakistan Under-19 at the ACC Men's U19 Asia Cup was always going to be the exception. The game on 8 November 2026 was played at the Al Amerat Cricket Ground near Muscat, and the timing suited Indian viewers almost perfectly.

Why Oman works for an Indian audience

Oman runs an hour and a half behind India. A 9:00 AM start in Muscat is 10:30 AM IST, so the toss falls around ten, the innings break lands in the early afternoon, and a full 100 overs finishes near 6:00 PM. Nobody has to set an alarm, and nobody misses the finish because of work. Compare that with India's senior tour of New Zealand, where the ODIs start before most of the country has had breakfast.

More than two points

On paper it was a Group A match in an eight-team tournament running from 5 to 14 November, with India, Pakistan, Nepal and the UAE in the same group. In practice it carried history. Pakistan arrived as defending champions, and the last time the two sides met, in the December 2025 final in Dubai, Pakistan won by 191 runs. India's side was led by Lakshya Raichandani, Pakistan's by Farhan Yousuf.

The ground

Every game of the tournament was played at one venue with two grass fields, known as Ministry Turf 1 and Ministry Turf 2. Listings disagreed on which of the two would host this fixture, which is a small but real headache for anyone trying to follow along. Morning starts also mean the side batting second gets the hotter part of a warm November day, and dry desert pitches tend to bring the spinners in once the ball softens.

Under-19 cricket is where many future India players first face real pressure in front of a crowd. A morning start, a full day of overs and an old rival make it worth following properly, from the first ball to the last.
